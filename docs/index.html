Scenario calculation

Asset change = value × (equity beta × equity shock − duration × rate shock − spread duration × credit spread shock) + derivative rate sensitivity × rate shock. A 0.02 rate shock means a 2 percentage point increase. These are first-order approximations. Derivative value and sensitivity are synthetic inputs.

Tactical index rebalancing

Ten synthetic positions start at 10% each. Raw weight = 1 + 0.5 × latest company sentiment, then normalize to 100%. GLOBAL events do not alter company weights. This demonstration does not claim trading performance.
